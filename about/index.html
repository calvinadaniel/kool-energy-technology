Our Story

SHAPED BY COMMERCIAL HVAC

Kool Energy Technology was founded to deliver professional-grade HVAC to New York's commercial market. That work — larger equipment, tighter tolerances, costly downtime, and stricter codes — still defines how we operate. The same team also takes on residential jobs for clients who want that commercial-grade standard at home.

Commercial HVAC is a different discipline. The equipment is larger, the stakes are higher, and the regulatory requirements are more demanding. Our technicians are trained and equipped for that environment, and we bring that experience to every property we serve.

We work with property managers, facility operators, business owners, building engineers, and homeowners across the Tri-State Area — the kind of reliable, transparent service commercial clients expect, applied wherever the job is.
