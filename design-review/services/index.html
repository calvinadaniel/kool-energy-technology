We designed two new ways to present your six services. Compare them with the current page, open each live preview to try it, and tell us which one you prefer.

A
Option A · Current page

Stacked rows

All six services appear one after another. Each row pairs a large photo with the description and the list of what's included, alternating sides as visitors scroll.

Works well for
Visitors who read the whole page, and showing off every field photo at full size.
Trade-off
The longest page, especially on phones. Services near the end are easy to miss.
Open live preview of option A (opens in a new tab)
B
Option B · New

Accordion

Services appear as a compact numbered list, each with a one-line preview of its specialties. Visitors open the ones they care about to see the full description, photo, and what's included. Several can be open at once, and Expand all shows everything.

Works well for
Scanning all six services at a glance, and keeping the page short on phones.
Trade-off
Photos and details stay hidden until a visitor opens a service.
Open live preview of option B (opens in a new tab)
C
Option C · New

Tabs

A numbered menu lists every service. Selecting one shows its photo and details in a large panel, and Next service steps through them in order. On phones, the menu becomes a strip that visitors swipe sideways.

Works well for
Focusing on one service at a time with the largest photography of the three options.
Trade-off
Only one service is visible at a time, so visitors must select each tab to compare.
