Plain Language

HVAC TERMS
EXPLAINED

Short definitions for the heating and cooling words that show up on our services page. If a term is new, start here.

HVAC

Heating, ventilation and air conditioning: the systems that heat, cool and move air in a building.

RTU (Rooftop Unit)

An all-in-one heating and cooling unit that sits on the roof, common on stores and offices.

Ductless mini split

A wall- or ceiling-mounted unit that heats and cools a room without ductwork.

VRF (Variable Refrigerant Flow)

One outdoor system that heats and cools many rooms separately, used in larger buildings. VRV is another name for the same kind of system.

Heat pump

Equipment that heats and cools by moving heat into or out of the building instead of burning fuel.

Gas-fired boiler

Heats water with natural gas, then sends hot water or steam to radiators or baseboards.

Gas furnace

Heats air with natural gas and blows it through the ducts.

BTU

A measure of heat. Equipment size is often listed in BTUs.

Ton

A measure of cooling capacity. One ton equals 12,000 BTUs per hour.

SEER2

An air conditioner's efficiency rating. Higher means lower electric bills.

AFUE

A furnace or boiler's efficiency: the percentage of fuel turned into heat.

Refrigerant

The fluid that carries heat in air conditioners and heat pumps.

EPA 608

The federal certification required to handle refrigerant.

IAQ (Indoor Air Quality)

How clean and healthy the air inside is: filtration, ventilation and humidity.

Economizer

A feature that uses cool outside air to cool a building when conditions allow, saving energy.

Preventive maintenance

Scheduled tune-ups that catch problems before they become breakdowns.

Load calculation

Working out how much heating and cooling a building needs, so equipment is sized right.
