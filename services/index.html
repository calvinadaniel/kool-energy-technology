What We Offer

OUR SERVICES

Full-spectrum commercial HVAC services for New York businesses. Every job handled by licensed, EPA-certified technicians.

01
Installation & Replacement

AIR CONDITIONING

Commercial AC systems demand precision engineering and industrial-grade equipment. We install, replace, and upgrade central air systems for office buildings, retail spaces, warehouses, and multi-unit commercial properties throughout New York.

  • — Central air system installation & replacement
  • — Rooftop unit (RTU) service & installation
  • — Multi-zone commercial system design
  • — Energy-efficiency upgrades & audits
02
Commercial Heating

HEATING SYSTEMS

Even in New York, commercial spaces require reliable heating solutions — particularly for cold storage, server rooms, and buildings with extreme occupancy swings. We design and install systems built for commercial-scale thermal management.

  • — Commercial heat pump installation
  • — Gas furnace systems for large spaces
  • — Heat strip & auxiliary heating solutions
  • — Zoned heating for mixed-use buildings
03
Ventilation & Filtration

INDOOR AIR QUALITY

Clean air is a compliance issue and a business asset. We assess, design, and implement IAQ systems that reduce allergens, control humidity, and meet ASHRAE and OSHA standards for commercial occupancy — keeping employees healthy and buildings code-compliant.

  • — HEPA & commercial filtration systems
  • — UV air purification & ionization
  • — Humidity control & dehumidification
  • — Ventilation design & ductwork assessment
04
Zone-Specific Cooling

DUCTLESS MINI SPLITS

When ductwork isn't practical or a space has unique temperature requirements, ductless mini-split systems deliver precise, efficient climate control without the infrastructure overhead. Ideal for server rooms, showrooms, additions, and retrofit projects.

  • — Single and multi-zone systems
  • — VRF/VRV commercial systems
  • — Ceiling cassette & concealed duct units
  • — Server room & IT closet climate control
05
Service Contracts

PREVENTATIVE MAINTENANCE

Unplanned downtime is expensive. Our scheduled maintenance contracts keep your HVAC systems running at peak efficiency year-round — reducing emergency repair costs, extending equipment lifespan, and keeping your building comfortable for tenants and employees.

  • — Quarterly & bi-annual service contracts
  • — Filter replacement & coil cleaning
  • — Refrigerant level checks & top-offs
  • — Full system diagnostics & reports
06
Specialty Applications

TEMPERATURE CONTROL SOLUTIONS

Some applications demand precise, narrow temperature tolerances. We design and install specialty climate systems for wine storage, pharmaceutical cold rooms, restaurant walk-in coolers, and data center cooling — anywhere standard HVAC falls short.

  • — Wine cellar & beverage storage systems
  • — Walk-in cooler & freezer climate systems
  • — Data center precision cooling
  • — Pharmaceutical & laboratory environments
